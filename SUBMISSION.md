## Title
Lumora

## Short description
Guide Nuri, a living spark, through sleeping lattices. Glide, awaken anchors, and restore quiet worlds.

## Long description
The Still Lattice has gone dark. Nuri, a small energy creature, can wake it — but only by gliding until the world itself says stop.

Swipe or press a direction. Nuri travels in a straight, smooth path, leaving a living wake. Anchors bloom as they pass. Wells drink the wake if Nuri rests inside them. Rifts unravel a careless line. Later worlds introduce seals, beacon order, drift vanes, and foldgates.

Objectives change from lattice to lattice. You are restoring a place, not covering a maze.

Earn Gleams for cosmetics only: pelts, wakes, skies, and completion blooms. A Dayweave lattice appears from the calendar date so every day has its own seeded puzzle.
